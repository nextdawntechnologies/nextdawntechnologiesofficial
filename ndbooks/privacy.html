Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026 · Published by Next Dawn Technologies

ND Books is an offline-first ebook reader. It doesn't require an account, sell data, or track you across apps.

We Do Not Collect Your Data

ND Books has no service that collects user data and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. The developer doesn't receive your books, notes, bookmarks, reading progress or settings. The App Store handles PRO payments; the app receives only a verified StoreKit entitlement and never your payment details.

Your Library Stays on Your Device

Books, original files, notes, bookmarks, reading positions, statistics and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Full backups are unencrypted ZIP files saved only where you choose.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you unlock PRO and turn on iCloud Sync, your books, reading positions, bookmarks and notes are stored in your own private iCloud database under your Apple Account. The developer can't access this data. Turning sync off keeps your local library and doesn't delete data from iCloud; you can manage it in your iCloud settings.

Wi-Fi Transfer Stays on Your Network

Local network access is used only while Wi-Fi transfer is running. Files go directly from your computer to your device over your local network, protected by a one-time access code, and never pass through our servers.

Listening and Text Recognition Stay on Your Device

Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS. Text recognition for scanned PDFs runs on your device with Apple's Vision framework; recognized text is cached on the device and isn't included in backups or sync.

You Control Your Data

You can delete books, notes and bookmarks at any time, and deleting the app removes its local data. Feedback emails contain only what you choose to send.

Policy Updates and Contact

If these practices change materially, we'll update this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact the developer at nextdawntechnologies@gmail.com.
